## Deploy via LiteRT

Streamline your deep learning workflow from training to on-device deployment.

[![Obtain Model Icon](https://developers.google.com/static/edge/litert/images/landing/obtain-model.svg)](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/collections)

### [1.Obtain a model](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/collections)

Use .tflite pre-trained models or convert PyTorch, JAX or TensorFlow models to .tflite.

[Explore models](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/collections)
[Learn about conversion](https://developers.google.com/edge/litert/conversion/overview)

[![Optimize Model Icon](https://developers.google.com/static/edge/litert/images/landing/optimize-model.svg)](https://developers.google.com/edge/litert/conversion/tensorflow/quantization/model_optimization)

### [2.Optimize](https://developers.google.com/edge/litert/conversion/tensorflow/quantization/model_optimization)

Use the LiteRT optimization toolkit to quantize your models post-training.

[Explore optimization](https://developers.google.com/edge/litert/conversion/tensorflow/quantization/model_optimization)

[![Run Model Icon](https://developers.google.com/static/edge/litert/images/landing/run-model.svg)](https://developers.google.com/edge/litert/overview#hardware-acceleration)

### [3.Run](https://developers.google.com/edge/litert/overview#hardware-acceleration)

Deploy your model with LiteRT and pick the optimal accelerator for your app.
