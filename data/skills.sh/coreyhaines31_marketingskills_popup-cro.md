**Popup and modal optimization for conversion without user friction or brand damage.**

* Covers seven popup types: email capture, lead magnets, discounts, exit intent, announcements, slide-ins, and feedback modals, each with specific copy and design formulas
* Includes five trigger strategies (time-based, scroll, exit intent, click, behavior) with guidance on when each works best and typical performance benchmarks
