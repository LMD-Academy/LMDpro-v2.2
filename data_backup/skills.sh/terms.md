Always review a skill's SKILL.md and source repository before installing it on a system that matters.

Use of the public API is rate-limited per IP. Programmatic abuse, scraping that bypasses the rate limit, or use that materially degrades service for others may result in IP-level blocks. Reasonable use, including caching results on your own infrastructure, is encouraged and not restricted.

See the [API documentation](/docs/api) for endpoint details.

## Removal and corrections

Skill authors who want a listing corrected, hidden, or redirected should follow the process documented on the [contact page](/contact). We honor reasonable takedown requests for skills that have been removed from their source repositories or that violate platform policies.
